The card allows customers to effortlessly make payments at POS terminals and for domestic online purchases on MPU E-Commerce websites. Enjoy interest-free usage until the repayment due date. In addition, can easily view and manage card activities through the Yoma Bank Next Mobile Application.

Important Information When Using FlexiPay MPU Credit Card

• Cardholders must not exceed the assigned credit limit.
• A monthly statement will be issued, including all card transactions and applicable fees.
• A minimum payment due may be set at 10% of the outstanding balance.
• The payment due date will be within 15 days from the statement issue date.
• A late payment fee of MMK 10,000 or 2% of the outstanding balance (whichever is higher) may be applied
if payment is not made on time.
• If the full outstanding balance is repaid, cardholders can enjoy up to 45 days of interest-free credit.
• An annual interest rate (e.g. 16%) will be charged on any outstanding balance not repaid by the due date.
• Repayments can be made conveniently through the Yoma Bank Next Mobile Application without visiting the bank.
