International Remittance

2C2P WAVE

2C2P WAVE is a secure and convenient international remittance service designed for Myanmar nationals living in Thailand to send money to their families back home. This service is integrated with Yoma Bank, ensuring fast and reliable transfers. With 2C2P WAVE, users in Thailand can transfer funds directly to Yoma Bank accounts in Myanmar at any time. Transactions can be monitored and managed through the Yoma Bank Next Application.

Available Services

Yoma Bank Account Direct Credit – Receive the money directly to your Yoma Bank Account

Wave Over the Counter – Withdraw cash at all Wave Money Agents with Mobile Number and Secret Code

Wave Wallet Transfer – Receive the money directly to your Wave Account

Note: Users in Thailand can download the 2C2P WAVE App and sign up via this link.

Other Information

Exchange Rates & Service Fees

Exchange rates and service fees are determined by 2C2P and may vary in real-time depending on market conditions.

Ria

Located in over 165 countries, Ria Money Transfer allows Receivers (beneficiaries) to withdraw cash easily at all Yoma Bank branches as well as to transfer directly to Yoma Bank accounts.

Western Union

Western Union allows receivers (beneficiaries) to withdraw cash at any Yoma Bank branch using a 10-digit MTCN (Money Transfer Control Number) and a government-issued ID.

Other Information

Foreign exchange rate is determined by Western Union’s system auto-calculation. WU’s FX rate may change from time to time.
