Trade Finance (International Banking)
Import and export are essential components of international trade, which is the exchange of goods and services between countries. This exchange of goods and services has been around for centuries, and it continues to be a significant factor in the growth and development of economies worldwide.
Exporting refers to the selling of goods and services from the home country to a foreign nation. Whereas, importing refers to the purchase of foreign products and bringing them into one’s home country.

Export and Import Services

Requires less investment in terms of time and money when contrasted with other methods of entering global trade.

It is comparatively less risky when compared with different routes of entering in international business.

As no nation can be 100% self-sufficient, import and export are very crucial for the functioning and growth of that nation.

Bank Guarantee

A Bank Guarantee (BG) is a written commitment by the bank (guarantor) to pay the beneficiary a certain sum of money within a specified period if the applicant (principal) fails to fulfill his contractual or other obligations of an underlying transaction. It is normally used to secure either a financial or performance obligation of the principal.

Deliverable Foreign Exchange Forward

A Deliverable Foreign Exchange Forward is a contract that protects customers against foreign exchange market risk by purchasing or selling foreign currency against Myanmar Kyat for later settlement at a predetermined rate. Deliverable this service can help you:

Focus on core business activities and reduce the need for monitoring foreign exchange fluctuations

Protect profit margins on imported and exported goods and services

Account for exchange rate fluctuations in budgeting and projections

Cross Currency Swap

In a Cross Currency Swap agreement, currency is not exchanged but temporarily swapped between bank and customer. This allows our customers to protect themselves against rate fluctuations by swapping one currency for another at current market rates with an agreement to re-swap later at a pre-agreed rate. Yoma Bank Cross Currency Swap is an effective tool for business with liabilities in one currency and revenue in another.

Currency Swap

A Currency Swap is similar to a Cross Currency Swap, but clients swap currency with a third party rather than with the bank directly. The currency is then re-swapped at a future date at a predetermined rate.
