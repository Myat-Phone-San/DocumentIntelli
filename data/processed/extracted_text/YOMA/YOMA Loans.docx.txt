MFI (Yoma Bank Back-to-Back Facility)

Yoma Bank’s Back-to-Back Facility is an effective way to protect microfinance institutions (MFIs) from currency fluctuations and foreign exchange risk. As a leader in MFI services, we strive to help the microfinance industry reach its full potential in Myanmar.

Expand your business with the support of Yoma Bank’s Back-to-Back Facility. Yoma Bank leads the way in lending to MFIs and aims to make the microfinance industry a long-term and stable contributor to the corporate business segment.
