Fixed Deposit Accounts

Fixed Deposit accounts offer competitive interest rates based on the chosen deposit option. Clients have the flexibility to select their preferred deposit duration, and upon maturity, they are entitled to the gained interest.
**If the deposit is withdrawn before the maturity period of the deposit is completed, you will not be entitled to enjoy the fixed interest. **

Better Life Deposit

With a 6-months or 1-year fixed deposit in Better Life Deposit, you can get life insurance worth up to 450,000 kyats from A Life Insurance, as well as medical expenses and other benefits combined with high interest rates. There are 6 types of packages for customers to choose from.

Life insurance policy will be issued and only Individual Account can be opened. If you want to open a joint, only (1) out of (2) people will get a life insurance policy.

Any Myanmar citizen between the ages of 18 and 60 can open.

In addition to life insurance coverage with a value according to the package you choose, you will be able to enjoy A Membership Privileges provided by A Insurance until the end of the insurance policy.
