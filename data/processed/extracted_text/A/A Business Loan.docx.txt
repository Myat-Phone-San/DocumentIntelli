Term Loan

Term loan is the most suitable for long-term investment as it can be enjoyed for 3 years.

Features

A term loan is a financial product offered by banks and financial institutions, providing borrowers with a lump sum of money that is repaid over a fixed term through scheduled installments.

Tenor - Loan tenor up to 3 years

Interest Rate - Affordable interest rate at 13% p.a.

Project Loan

The verification of the loan is contingent on the Project Plan, depending on short-term and long-term projects. The loan lifespan can be varied, aligning with the unique characteristics of each project.

Features

Loans that are verified according to the Project Plan

Interest Rate - 13% p.a.

Tenor - Tenor can be discussed and extended as project timeline

Overdraft Loan

Overdraft Loan is short-term loan for businesspeople allowing them to loan money only when the money is needed.

Features

An overdraft loan is a flexible financing facility offered by banks, allowing customers to withdraw funds beyond their account balance up to an approved limit. It acts as a short-term borrowing option to manage liquidity and meet urgent financial needs.

Tenor - Up to 1 year

Interest Rate - 13% p.a.
