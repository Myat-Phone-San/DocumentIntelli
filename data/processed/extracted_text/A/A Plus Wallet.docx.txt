Features of A Plus

With A+ Wallet, you can: Make card payments and manage your savings account, Transfer large amounts (over 100 lakhs MMK) to your A bank savings account, Send money to other local bank accounts with low service fees, Top up phone credit, pay internet bills, or plan travel expenses — all in one app.

Take full control of your finances and lifestyle — all in one place — with the smart, seamless experience of A+ Wallet.

Transfer

With A+ Wallet, you can easily transfer money to your friends and contacts.
There are 4 ways to send money from your A+ account:

1. Transfer between A+ accounts – Simply use the “Transfer” feature in the A+ Wallet app for instant and easy transfers.

2. Transfer to non-A+ users – Send money by providing the phone number that received the transfer, the amount of money, and the cash code, with a service fee based on the amount.

3. Transfer to other local bank accounts – You can send money directly from your A+ account to other local banks.

4. Transfer via agent – For users without an A+ account, fill in their name, phone number, and NRC number to transfer through the nearest A+ agent.

Choose the method that suits you best and transfer with ease!

Split Bill Payment

Split Bill Payment is a feature that allows you to evenly divide and settle a bill based on the number of people involved.
To use the Split Bill feature:

1. Go to the “Split Bill” section from the main page

2. Enter the total bill amount

3. Tap on “Split Evenly” and simply enter the A+ phone numbers of the people you’re splitting the bill with.

What kind of MDR rate does A+ Merchant charge?

Merchant shops will be charged a standard MDR rate of 0.5% per transition. If you start applying as A+ merchant, the MDR rate for the first year is waived from the date of application.

As an A+ merchant, what is the maximum cash withdrawal amount?

As an A+ merchant, if you want to withdraw the cash, you must transfer the money from your e-wallet to the A+ bank account which is linked with A+ merchant account. After switching to your linked account, you can fully withdraw 100% of your cash at the agent withdrawal counter located at the nearest bank branch.

What benefits can I get as an A+ merchant?

If you become an A+ merchant, you can participate in Specific Promotion Campaigns made by A Plus Wallet from time to time. By adding advertisements to the channels made by A Plus, the store can gain brand awareness.

What are the bill payments that are linked with A+ Wallet?

Currently, the bill payment services available are ANANDA, Hi Wifi, MyanmarNet AwaThone, Myanmar Net Data, and Myanmar Net Prepaid.
