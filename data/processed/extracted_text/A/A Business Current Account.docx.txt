Foreign Currency Account

Individuals utilizing foreign currencies, such as business leaders, companies, and those receiving salaries in foreign currencies (e.g., seafarers), can open an account with a minimum deposit of 100 units in their preferred currency, choosing from USD, EURO, SGD, THB, and CNY. Specifically, for USD, EURO, and SGD accounts, a minimum of 10 units is required, while THB and CNY accounts should have a minimum of 100 units in their balances.

Features

You can open an account with a minimum deposit of foreign currency.
