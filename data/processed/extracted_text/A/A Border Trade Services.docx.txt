Border Trade Services

THB-MMK Border Trade

Thailand-Myanmar Border Trade and China-Myanmar Border Trade Services can be performed at A bank for faster operational payments.

Services available with Company Current accounts include:

Top of Form

Bottom of Form

Performing transfers (INWARD TT) and OUTWARD TT

Informing the Import Letter of Credit and export Letter of Credit

Documentary Collection

A bank branches that can obtain the Border Trade Service are:

• Bo Aung Kyaw branch bank

• War Tan branch bank

• Natmauk branch bank

• Mandalay branch bank (1)

• Muse branch bank

• Myawadi branch bank

Bayint Naung branch bank

• Shwegone Daing branch bank

• University Avenue branch bank

• Tachilake branch Bank

• Myeik branch bank

• Kawthoung branch bank

* Currency exchange rates will be set daily.
