Features

Secure your savings with guaranteed returns. Fixed Deposit Accounts offer higher interest rates, flexible tenures, and risk-free growth to help you achieve your financial goals with confidence.

Term - You can choose the term of deposit you want to collect.

Better Life Deposit

With a 6-month or 1-year fixed deposit in Better Life Deposit, you can get life insurance worth up to 450,000 kyats from A Life Insurance, as well as medical expenses and other benefits combined with high interest rates. There are 6 types of packages for customers to choose from. Life insurance policy will be issued and only Individual Account can be opened. If you want to open a joint, only (1) out of (2) people will get a life insurance policy. Any Myanmar citizen between the ages of 18 and 60 can open. In addition to life insurance coverage with a value according to the package you choose, you will be able to enjoy A Membership Privileges provided by A Insurance until the end of the insurance policy.
