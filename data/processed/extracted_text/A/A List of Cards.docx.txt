Features

MPU Debit Card is a type of debit card provided by some banks or financial institutions, typically in regions like Myanmar, though similar cards can be available in other countries. The MPU stands for Myanmar Payment Union, and it is a platform aimed at facilitating the electronic payment system for financial institutions and consumers.

A bank MPU Debit ကတ်ကိုအခမဲ့ လျှောက်ထားနိုင်ပြီး နှစ်စဥ်ကြေးလည်း ပေးဆောင်ရန် မလိုပါ။
