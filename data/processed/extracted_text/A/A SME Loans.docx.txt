Agriculture Loan

The Agriculture Loan can be applied for financing investments and acquiring other necessary resources. This specialized loan is designed to support the development of various activities, including livestock, agriculture, and local product assistance.

The Agriculture Loan is a specialized financial product provided by banks to support farmers, agribusinesses, and agricultural projects. These loans help finance various agricultural activities such as buying seeds, fertilizers, machinery, irrigation systems, livestock, and other farming-related needs.
