Home To Cash (H2C) Loan

Need quick cash loan for sudden expenses or any personal purposes?
Are you a present Home Loan customer of AYA Bank?
Then, turn your home to quick cash by applying AYA Home to Cash (H2C) loan for your financial needs up to the amount of home loan repaid principal.

Features

Loan Amount | MMK 5,000,000 to MMK 1,000,000,000 (up to the amount of repaid principal)
Loan Tenor | 6 months to 120 months (up to the remaining tenor of present home loan)
Interest Rate | 14.5% per annum
Service Charge | 2% of principal loan

Note:Customers can apply H2C loan only at the branch where they applied for the home loan.

Eligibility

Existing AYA Home Loan customer

Minimum 24 consecutive months of Home Loan has been paid back to the Bank

Minimum of 6 months remaining on your home loan

Primary Applicant must have stable and reasonable monthly income
