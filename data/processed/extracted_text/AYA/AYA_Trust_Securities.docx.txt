Summary of AYA Trust Securities

AYA Trust Securities Official Website

AYA Trust Securities (AYA Trust) is the securities and investment banking arm of the AYA Financial Group and one of Myanmar's licensed securities companies. It provides investment, stock trading, and corporate finance services to both individual and institutional investors.

Key Information

Founded/License Date: Licensed by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Myanmar on 1 March 2016 under License No. 003.

Head Office: No. 416, Mahabandoola Road & Mahabandoola Garden Street, Kyauktada Township, Yangon, Myanmar.

Parent Group: Part of the AYA Financial Group, alongside AYA Bank and AYA SOMPO Insurance.
