Products and Services

AYA Pay enables customers to pay a wide variety of bills through its platform, offering a seamless and efficient process for managing recurring payments. Whether you’re paying online through the app or offline at an agent, AYA Pay ensures quick and secure transactions. Here’s a breakdown of the biller services available:

Utility Bill Payments

Customers can easily pay their utility bills using AYA Pay’s services. This includes:

Electricity Bills
Pay your electricity bills by providing your meter number or account number through the app or at an AYA Pay Agent.

Internet Subscriptions

AYA Pay supports payment for internet service providers. Customers can pay their monthly internet subscriptions by sharing their customer ID or account number with the agent or through the app.

Telecom Payments

Mobile Topups
Recharge your phone or purchase data bundles by simply sharing your phone number with the AYA Pay Agent or through the AYA Pay app.

Postpaid Bills
Postpaid customers can clear their monthly telecom service bills directly from their AYA Pay account or at an agent location.

Loan Repayment

Microfinance Repayments
Customers with microfinance loans or bank loans can easily repay their installments using AYA Pay. All you need is your loan account number or reference number.

TV and Entertainment Subscription Payments

Cable TV and Digital Subscription Services
Pay for popular TV subscriptions like cable and satellite services by providing your subscriber account number.

Insurance Payments

Insurance Premium Payments
AYA Pay also allows users to settle their monthly or annual insurance premium payments with participating insurance companies. Provide your policy number to the agent or enter it into the app.

Education Payments

School and University Fee Payments
customers will be able to pay tuition fees for participating schools and universities via AYA Pay, making education payments more convenient.
