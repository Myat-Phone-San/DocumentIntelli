Personal Loan

You don’t have to use your savings or you don’t need a collateral, in times of emergencies or to meet your different requirements. We offer AYA Personal loan, a short-term loan, for customers using our payroll service. Our hassle-free, online application process helps you apply for personal loan instantly within minutes.

Features

Loan Tenor | minimum 1 month to maximum 12 months
Interest Rate | 14.5% per annum
