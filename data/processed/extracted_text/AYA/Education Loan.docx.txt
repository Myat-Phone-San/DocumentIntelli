Education Loan

To meet your child’s learning goals, AYA Education Loan provides financial support for completing certificate, diploma, degree and post graduate programs in local and foreign countries. No financial worries to embark on education journey and get financing from AYA with or without collaterals.

Features

Apply at any AYA branch with 0% down-payment

Repayment by fixed monthly installment plan.

Parents (or guardians) can apply on behalf of students who do not have income.

Secured loan customers providing collaterals are eligible to apply at schools, colleges and universities either local or foreign country.

Unsecured loan customers are offered to apply from our partner institutions - local private bordering schools, colleges, universities, and international Schools. Explore options available to position your child’s education journey with our partner institutions

Eligibility

Students eligible to apply for the loan include:

High school students

Undergraduate students

Graduates planning to continue their studies

Students without regular monthly income are required to apply jointly with a legal guardian or parent who:

Is a Myanmar Citizen

Has a regular monthly income, and

Is not over 59 years of age at the time of application

Upon your successful submission of AYA Education Loan Application Form along with required documents to the bank, approval process could take up to 5 Working days. Once approved and you agree on the terms and conditions of the AYA Education Loan, you will be contacted to visit AYA Bank with your guarantor to deposit the required down payment for the program fees.
