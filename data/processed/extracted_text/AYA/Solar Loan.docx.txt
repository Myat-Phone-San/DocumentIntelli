Solar Loan

Power your home with solar system and enjoy immediate energy cost savings with renewable energy. AYA Bank is committed to helping you make the switch with confidence! Purchasing of solar board and related costs can be applied at AYA Bank with affordable monthly payment plan.

Features

Down Payment | Minimum 30%
Interest Rate | 13% per annum
Loan Tenor | Up to 2 years
Loan Amount | Maximum MMK 30,000,000 (Thirty Million Kyats)
Service Charges | • 1% for loan tenor 1 year; • 2% for loan tenor 2 years

Eligibility

You Must be a Myanmar Citizen

You must have regular monthly income

You must be between 18 and 59 years of age

You must purchase from the authorized dealer partnered with AYA Bank

Terms and Conditions

It is compulsory to fulfill the requirement of making minimum down payment, service charges and other applicable fees related to bank’s facilities before purchasing the solar system.

Bank shall approve the final loan amount based on the selling price mentioned on the Sales and Purchase agreement. Any other additional costs will not be financed by the bank.
