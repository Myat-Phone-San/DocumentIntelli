What is the difference between AYA Mobile 2.0 and the new AYA Mobile Banking app?

The new AYA Mobile Banking app is a major upgrade from AYA Mobile 2.0, designed to be faster, more secure, and more convenient. In addition to the core services like transfers, bill payments, and card management, the new app introduces a range of powerful new features.

Unified Transfer Limit - Send larger amounts without switching to internet banking.

AYA Pay Integration - Instant wallet transfers without fees.

Save Payees - Quicker repeat transfers to other AYA bank accounts and AYA Pay wallets.

Transaction PIN - Security and ease of use are improved, replacing OTPs.

Manage Devices - View and manage your trusted devices and login sessions.

Card Security - Your cards are now automatically linked and can be instantly locked or unlocked if lost.

Seamless Loan Feature - Track progress and make loan repayments all in one place.

Digital Fixed Deposits - Open Digital Fixed Deposit accounts with special rates.

ATM Status Checker - Check nearby ATM availability before heading out.

Live Chat - Built-in live chat support for 9 am to 5 pm daily.

With its modern design and these new capabilities, the new AYA Mobile Banking app delivers a more seamless and user-friendly banking experience.

What types of loan can I manage in the new AYA mobile banking app and where can I see my loan repayment details such as outstanding amount?

Currently in the new AYA Mobile Banking app, you can view Home Loans and Auto Loans as well as make loan repayments. To view your loan repayment details, select any existing loan in the Loan Repayment feature.

How can I manage my debit cards, credit cards and prepaid cards in the new AYA mobile banking app?

Debit Cards, Credit Cards, Prepaid Cards and Buy Now Pay Later Cards will automatically appear in the new AYA Mobile Banking app. You can manage them in the Cards page. You can lock and unlock your cards, view card details, top up your prepaid cards, and make credit card repayments.

What will happen when the saving period of my digital Fixed Deposit account ends?

This depends on the type of Digital Fixed Deposit account you have opened.

Upfront: Saving period will auto-renew after current one ends.

Maturity: You will receive your full deposit and interest credited to your respective accounts.

How can I use the Secured Device Management Feature in the new AYA mobile banking app?

The new AYA Mobile Banking app allows you to easily view and manage all devices that have accessed your AYA Mobile Banking account through the Device Management feature. You can see which devices have logged in to your account and remotely log out of unfamiliar devices for your security.
