Kyee Pwar Chan Thar

The Kyee Pwar Chan Thar Saving Plan is a financial option designed specifically for individuals between the ages of 18 and 45 who are looking to secure both the benefits of a savings account and life insurance.

The life insurance protection benefits include coverage for death, total permanent disability, and dismemberment resulting from accidents, based on the targeted savings amount. Additionally, savers can also enjoy a competitive annual interest rate of up to 5% on their bank deposits.

This plan is available in two options, the Kyee Pwar Savings Plan for customers aged 18 to 35 years and the Chan Thar Savings Plan for customers aged 36 to 45 years. Both plans can be conveniently purchased through uabpay, making it easier for individuals to manage their finances and secure their future.

Kyee Pwar Savings Plan

Three types – Life Savings, Life Savings Extra (Essential), Life Savings Extra (Holistic)

Life Savings

Initial Deposit – 10,000 MMK
Monthly Minimum Deposit – 5,000 MMK

The Life Savings Plan offers significant life insurance benefits for up to 36 months based on total savings.

Coverage and Benefits

Earning a bonus based on the deposit balance.

Monthly bonus of up to 5% p.a will be calculated daily and added to uabpay every month.

Financial protection

By depositing 100,000 Kyat per month with Life Savings, enjoy the following benefits.:

Accidental death/ total permanent disability – Kyats 15,000,000

Non-accidental Death/ Total Permanent Disability – Kyats 7,500,000

Accidental Dismemberment Benefit- Kyat 3,750,000

Regular Monthly Savings

Minimum monthly savings start from 5,000 kyats.

Short Term Savings

Medium-term savings plan type for 36 months only.

Indestructible Financial Protection

If the monthly savings plan is terminated, insurance protection will be sustained for a period of 36 months.

Ease to resume the plan

The savings plan can be extended for up to six (6) months by collecting the total amount as a lump sum for the months of absence. Interest will not be calculated for the absence months.

Ease to cancel the plan

The initial deposit and the first month’s savings of the savings plan will be charged as service charges and the remaining savings balance will be automatically transferred to the uabpay wallet A/C.

Minimum Balance 1,000 MMK

Eligible Currency accounts – MMK, USD, EUR, SGD

Individual or Joint

Cheque Book available

Benefits

Convenient and reliable for regular payments and receivables.

Can transfer funds from one account to another through SaiSai Pay, uabpay+.

Access to current account is available through mobile application, online banking.

A Cheque book is provided to the customer to allow an easy access for payment.
