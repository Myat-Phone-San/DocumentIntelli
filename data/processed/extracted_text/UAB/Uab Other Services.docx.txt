Hedging Services

It’s a solution that helps you mitigates foreign currencies risks and assures you gain benefits from competitive foreign exchange values. Our team of dedicated professional will help you understand your exposure and develop personalized protection strategies.

FX Forward

FX Forward is a contract in which the specific rate is locked to avoid volatility of the foreign exchange market by purchasing or selling foreign currency against Myanmar Kyat for later settlement at a predetermined rate.

Cross Currency Swap

In a Cross Currency Swap agreement, currency is not exchanged but temporarily swapped between bank and customer. This allows our customers to protect themselves against rate fluctuations by swapping a quantity of one currency for another at current market rates with an agreement to re-swap later at a pre-agreed rate.

2. Types of Orders and Order Execution

Limit Order

Allows purchase or sale of a security at a specified price or better:

Buy limit order: Order will be executed only at the limit price or lower

Sell limit order: Order will be executed only at the limit price of higher

Market Order

An order placed without specifying the buying price or selling price

Block Trade

Orders over 10,000 units can buy and sell with the negotiated price through uab securities

Before Placing an order

There must be enough cash in your trading bank account to fund a buy order and sufficient quantity of shares in your securities trading account to make a sell order. Without sufficient cash and shares, order will be rejected by the System

Placing an order

You can also place an order through our broker. Simply call your Marketing Officer at the dedicated telephone number and your order will be processed without delay. Alternatively, you can visit our head office to place orders

Sustainability Tool

At uab bank, we believe that sustainability is no longer optional ,  it is essential for building resilient businesses and communities. In partnership with ESGpedia, we have introduced a Sustainability Tool to help enterprises in Myanmar and beyond evaluate their sustainability readiness.

The tool provides your company with a starting point to understand where you stand in the sustainability journey and how you can align with global sustainability standards, while creating long-term value for your stakeholders.

Features & Benefits​

Sustainability Tool empowers businesses to assess and strengthen their sustainability practices. Through this tool, businesses gain valuable insights into their current sustainability readiness and can identify opportunities for improvement.

By completing the assessment, businesses will:

Receive guidance on sustainability strengths and improvement areas.

Gain recognition badges that highlight their sustainability commitment.​

Increase visibility with investors, customers, and partners looking for responsible businesses. ​

What is the Sustainability Tool?

It is a digital platform designed to help businesses measure their sustainability readiness by assessing the sustainability pillars. The results will help organisations understand their current standing and areas of growth.

Why should business use the Sustainability Tool?

The tool is designed for enterprises of all sizes from SMEs taking their first steps into sustainability, to corporates strengthening their sustainability strategies.

What is the cost?

The Sustainability Tool is free to use for all businesses. Upon completion, a personalized report will be issued, providing insights to support sustainability planning and reporting.

What is ESBN?

The ESCAP Sustainable Business Network (ESBN) is a United Nations initiative that connects businesses across the Asia-Pacific region to collaborate on sustainable development and advance the UN 2030 Agenda. It brings together business leaders, policymakers, and innovators to promote inclusive, responsible, and green business practices that contribute to achieving the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

The ESBN Green Deal is a digital platform that helps companies measure, improve, and showcase their sustainability performance through data insights and assessment tools. Businesses, including those in Myanmar, can benefit by benchmarking their ESG performance, gaining international recognition through Green, Silver, or Gold badges, and accessing regional networks and resources that support sustainable growth, competitiveness, and global visibility

For more information of badges, please visit: ESBN Asia-Pacific Green Deal Badge Achievers
