Short Term Endowment Life Insurance

Short Term Endowment life insurance aims to provide financial assistance in case of unanticipated events as well as to save a predetermined amount of money after a certain period.

What you get!

Financial security

Good savings habit

Tax saving plan

Key Features

Policy Term: 5 years, 7 years, 10 years (As you choose)

Premium Term: 5 years, 7 years, 10 years

Get started with 1,000,000 Kyats or up to 50,000,000 Kyats

Insurable Age: 10 – 60 years old

Benefits

Death

Maturity

Total Permanent Disability

Exclusions

Death

• Due to voluntarily committing suicide within one year of policy term.
• Due to undisclosed pre-existing illness or disease within one year of policy term.

Total Permanent Disability

• Intentional self-harm
• Suicide attempt
• Abuse of narcotic drugs
• Take illegal medicine/tablets
• Involvement in a criminal case.
• Undisclosed pre-existing illness or disease
