Card+

CB Card+ is a dedicated mobile application from CB Bank  that lets you manage and use your CB Bank credit and prepaid cards digitally from your phone. To use CB Card+ app, all you need is a compatible NFC-enabled Android phone and CB Bank credit and prepaid cards. You can make payment as simple as tapping with your phone wherever Visa Contactless Payment is accepted.

Card+ Features

Android (NFC – enabled)

Users can check available promotions to enjoy better deals.

Customers can tap and pay securely wherever Visa Contactless is accepted.

The app allows users to block and unblock their cards instantly for security.

Users can top up their prepaid cards easily through the app.

Credit card holders can make credit card repayments conveniently.

Customers can view detailed information about their cards at any time.

Users can review their recent card transactions.

Customers can enquire and view their card statements whenever needed.

Personal Internet Banking

Personal Internet Banking at CB Bank is an online service that lets you manage your bank account and perform many everyday banking tasks securely through a web browser without needing to visit a branch.

It’s designed for individual customers with CB Bank accounts.

Personal Internet Banking is a secure online portal you access with your username, password, and OTP/security device that lets you connect to your CB Bank account from a computer, tablet, or smartphone anywhere you have internet and 24/7 access.

Personal Internet Banking Features

Checking balances

View account history

Loan schedule

Manage beneficiary

Bill payments

Fund transfer to own account or to other CB Bank account or Other Bank

Mobile E-top up

Easi travel card top up

Credit card bill payment

Purchases

International Payment (non-trade)

Payment stop request

Cheque book request

Business Internet Banking

Business Internet Banking at CB Bank is the bank’s online banking platform built specifically for business and corporate customers.

It lets companies manage their business finances and perform banking transactions securely via the internet, using a computer, tablet, or any device with a web browser, without having to visit a branch.

CB Bank’s Business Internet Banking (also called Business iBanking) is a secure digital banking portal that enables businesses to access and manage their bank accounts and conduct banking operations online 24/7.

It is tailored with features that support business needs, including multi-user control and transaction approval workflows.

Business Internet Banking Features

Check account balances

View account history and transaction records

Review loan schedules and future-dated enquiries

Fund transfers (to own accounts or other CB Bank accounts)

Manage beneficiaries for safer payments

Stop payments or cheque services

Standing orders for recurring transfers

Bill payments and mobile e-top-ups

Payroll services to pay employees

Bulk payments to multiple suppliers at once

International payments (trade and non-trade)

Transfers to other local banks (with beneficiary setup)
