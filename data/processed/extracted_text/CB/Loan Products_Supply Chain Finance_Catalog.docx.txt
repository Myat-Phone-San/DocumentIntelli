Distributor Financing

It is provided to Anchor/Sponsor’s distributors to meet their floor plans and primary sales.

The program is based on Anchor’s value chain strength and interdependency between them, particularly dealer/distributors livelihood and cash flows are directly related to the Anchor's product strength and market demand, providing clear direction of future business growth of distributor.

Business Eligibility & Tenure

Applications can be processed at HO and all CB Bank branches through Zone RMs.

Standard requirement is a minimum of 3 years of business operations.

Customers with good reputations and high creditworthiness may be considered with a minimum of 1 year business length.

Eligible for businesses holding a valid Company/Business License with consistent tax records.

For Distributor Financing, borrowers must have served as a representative of a Large Corporation for at least 1 year.

SME Member Card is not mandatory, but presenting one increases approval chances.

Collateral

Land and Buildings or Condominiums are accepted as collateral.

Highly creditworthy customers may have the opportunity to apply on a non-collateral basis.

Repayment Scheme & Loan Tenure

Operates as a Revolving Credit Facility.

Tenor ranges from a minimum of 30 days up to 180 days.

100% of the financing amount must be paid upon the maturity date.

Loan processing timeline requires a minimum of 1 month.

Interest Rate and Charges

Facility Fee: 1% on credit limit (Upfront on the date of facility agreement).

Processing Fee: 1% on financing amount upon each transaction.

Interest Rate Range: 10% to 14.5% p.a. (determined by collateral or non-collateral status).

Required Documents List for Distributor Financing

CB Bank Distributor Financing Application Form.

Distributor’s Business Profile and Background.

Past Sales Contracts & Invoices between Supplier and Buyer.

Certificate of Incorporation and Company Extract from DICA or Business License.

Financial Reports for the last 2 years.

Income Tax, Municipal Tax & Commercial Tax Slips for the last 2 years.

Collateral-related documents (if applicable).

Invoice Financing

It is transaction based, where actual sale and purchase of goods and/or services are involved on an open account basis, corporate (anchor) buys from their suppliers.

Financing is provided to suppliers as a source of early payment.

It focuses on the underlying relationship between buyer and seller and finance that part of transaction which has strong linkage with anchor supply chain.

Required Documents List for Invoice Financing

CB Bank Invoice Financing Application Form.

Past Sales Contracts & Invoices between Supplier and Buyer for 1 year.

Corporate Profile (with 3 photos) and Business History of the Supplier.

Board of Directors (BOD) Resolution for the Invoice Financing application.

Certificate of Incorporation, Company Extract from DICA, Director’s NRC copies, and Census list.

Full Financial Reports (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement) for the last 3 years.

Income Tax & Commercial Tax Slips for the last 2 years.

Undertaking Letter from the Buyer for payment on the invoice due date.

Collateral-related documents (if applicable).

Reverse Factoring

It is a type of supplier finance solution that companies can use to offer early payments to their suppliers based on approved invoices.

Suppliers participating in a reverse factoring program can request early payment on invoices from the bank or other finance provider, with the buyer sending payment to the financial institution on the invoice maturity date.

By giving suppliers access to reverse factoring, buyers can reduce the risk of disruption in their supply chains and strengthen their supplier relationships, while also improving their own working capital position.
