Are there any limitations on outward payment transactions?

Trade payments – No limit, provided all supporting documents are complete.

Non-trade payments – Require prior approval from FESC.

________________________________________

How long does it take for funds to reach overseas?

Depositary Correspondent Bank: Same day (T+0) if submitted before cut-off time.

After cut-off time: Next working day (T+1).

________________________________________

Can advance payments be remitted?

No, advance payments are not allowed.

________________________________________

Do I need to submit KYC documents for every transaction?

No. If you are a CB Bank account holder, you do not need to submit KYC documents for each transaction.

Only supporting documents relevant to the purpose of payment are required.

________________________________________

Can I remit funds if I don’t have a Foreign Currency (FCY) Account with CB Bank?

No. Customers must hold a Foreign Currency Account with CB Bank for overseas remittance.

________________________________________

When will my transaction be processed?

Requests received before 12:00 noon on a working day: Processed on the same day (if account balance, signature, and documents are complete).

Requests received after 12:00 noon: Processed on the next working day.

Can a customer cancel a fund transfer request?

Yes, bank will try to do on a best-effort basis.

Requires a signed customer request letter.

Cancellation charge: USD 10 (or equivalent in MMK).

________________________________________

Can a customer amend a fund transfer request (e.g., beneficiary name, account number, address, or payment details)?

Yes, on a best-effort basis.

Requires a signed customer request letter.

Amendment charge: USD 10 (or equivalent in MMK).

International & Local Trade Services Division

Q.1. Who can apply for opening of LC with CB Bank?

A1. Importers with an account at CB Bank can apply in the prescribed format with supporting documents. LC will be issued against 100% cash margin or approved Trade Facility.

Q.2  What is the commission for issuance of LC?

A.2.  0.25% per quarter plus cable charges USD 5, subject to minimum USD 50 and maximum USD 2000.

Q.3 What are the documents required for opening LC?

A.3

1. Proforma Invoice or Sale Contract

2. LC Application with duty stamp

3. Request Letter on company letterhead

4. Import License (if applicable)

5. Company Registration & Extract (if new customer).

If importers want to use his export money, this company must pay export classification with company letter head.

If importers want to use existing balance, this company must pay existing balance with company letter head.

Q.4 How do I apply for Import LC?

A.4 Download the LC Application Form from CB Bank’s website or collect from Trade Centers. Submit completed form with company signatures and stamp. A trade facility or 100% margin deposit is required.

Q.5 Can the importer apply online for LC?

A.5 Yes. Forms can be downloaded from CB Bank’s website for frequent users.

Q.6 How do I obtain an Import LC without trade facilities?

A.6 The Bank may issue an LC against 100%–110% cash margin deposit depending on Incoterms.

Q.7 Do I get a copy of the LC after application?

A.7 Yes. A copy will be provided via mail or collected at CB Bank Trade Services Counter as per client’s request.

Q.8 What types of LCs can CB Bank issue?

A.8 Sight and Usance/Deferred Payment LCs; Negotiation/Payment and Acceptance LCs in local and foreign currencies.

Q.9 In which currencies can the LC be opened?

A.9 USD, EUR, SGD, THB, CNY, JPY, INR.

Q.10 What restrictions apply for LC issuance?

A.10 Issuance subject to KYC/DD/AML checks. Bank may refuse issuance if compliance or legal restrictions apply.

Q.11 How can I clear cargo without original documents?

A.11 Apply for a Shipping Guarantee (SG) from CB Bank to release cargo and avoid demurrage until original documents arrive.

Q.12 How do I apply for Shipping Guarantee?

A.12 Submit SG Application, Company Letterhead, Transport Document, Invoice, and required margin (typically 10% or 110% of LC amount).

Q.13 What should I do once I receive Original BL/AWB after SG?

A.13 For BL: return BL to shipping company in exchange for indemnity, return indemnity to CB Bank. For AWB: no return needed.

Q.14 What is the commission for Shipping Guarantee?

A.14 0.25% of bill amount per 3 months (Min USD 50).

Q.15 When will the SG margin be refunded?

A.15 Refund is made once CB Bank receives original shipping documents.

Q.16 How will I be notified of Import Collection documents?

A.16 You will receive notice by mail/phone from CB Bank.

Q.17 What is the process for settlement under D/P and D/A?

A.17

D/P: Pay at sight or use Trust Receipt financing.

D/A: Sign Bill of Exchange and settle at maturity either by account debit or TR financing.

Q.18 What documents are required for Import Bill release?

A.18 Request Letter on company letterhead, Import License (if applicable), Company Registration & Extract (if new customer).

Q.19 What is the charge for handling Import Collection Bill?

A.19  0.25% of bill amount (Min USD 50) + USD 5 cable charges.

Q.20 How do I apply for Export Documentary Collection?

A.20 Submit completed Documentary Collection Form, Bill of Exchange, and shipping documents to CB Bank Trade Center.

Q.21 What documents are required for Export Documentary Collection?

A.21 Documentary Collection Form, Request Letter, Bill of Exchange with shipping docs, Export License (if applicable), Export Declaration, Company Registration & Extract (if new customer) , MOC Approval ( If applicable ).

Q.22 What are the charges for Export Bill Collection?

A.22 0.25% of bill amount (Min USD 50) + USD 5 cable charges.

Q.23 How will I be notified of arrival of Export LC?

A. You will receive notice by mail/phone from CB Bank.

Q.24 What documents are required to collect Original Export LC?

Q.27 What are the charges for Export Bill under LC?

Q7. Can I transfer money from abroad into a Yuan account and keep it in my account? If not, what percentage must I sell through Online Trade?
A7.

Non-Trade Transactions:

If the company is registered under MIC, it is allowed to keep the funds.

If the company is not registered under MIC, it is not allowed to keep the funds. The full amount (100%) must be converted at the CBM rate (not the online rate).

Export Trade Transactions:

If the company is MIC-registered and the export commodity is agricultural or marine products, funds cannot be kept for more than one month. At least 25% must be sold upon credit to the account, while the remaining 75% may be sold at the online rate.

If the export commodity is not agricultural or marine products, funds may be retained.

If the company is a local entity, funds cannot be kept for more than one month. At least 25% must be sold upon credit to the account, while the remaining 75% may be sold at the online rate.

Q8. Can I receive funds from abroad into a Company Account or an FCY Account?
A8. Yes. Can receive funds from abroad as per CBM Guidelines . For more details please contact CB Bank Trade Center .

Q9. Is there a limit on the amount I can receive from abroad via SWIFT?
A9. No. There is no upper limit for credits to your MMK account after conversion of foreign currency.
