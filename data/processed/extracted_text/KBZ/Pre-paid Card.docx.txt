Pre-Paid Card

KBZ EXPLORE Prepaid Card is available in both VISA & MasterCard.
KBZ Explore Prepaid Cards is suitable for travelers to foreign countries, students going abroad for further studies and those who want to make online payments.
This prepaid card is accepted worldwide, including online merchants and ATMs. You can easily check your card transactions and balance by logging into your KBZ online account. Apply for an instant prepaid card at selected branch only.

Using your card: The cardholders can enjoy the discounts from many hotels, restaurants and shopping centers overseas depending on their types of cards.

Safety: The card embedded with new technology EMV Smart Chip can fully prevent the chances of your card information being cloned for fraud and illegal transactions.

Making payments with your card: You can use the amount deposited in your card (after withholding USD 20 (OR) 20,000 MMK at least in your card)(maximum per day). You cannot transfer money between card wallets.
