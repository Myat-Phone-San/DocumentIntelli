Wedding Saving Account

The Wedding Savings Account is perfect for couples who are planning to get married in the future. This account enables them to save for their big day without worrying about the financial burden that comes with it by allowing them to save money on a regular basis without having to invest a large sum of money upfront. With this account, customers can choose their preferred monthly saving amount and targeted saving goals that suits their budget and financial goals.

Benefits

Great savings option to attain your goal

Able to save monthly steady amount

Guaranteed returns

Better rate of interest

Special offers and promotions from partnered merchants

Features

Terms of Deposit and Interest- 10% 1 year

*Effective as of September 1st, 2024

Saving Frequency- Monthly

Minimum Monthly Saving Amount- 30,000 MMK

Maximum Monthly Saving Amount- Unlimited

Product Highlights

Opening An Account- Account opening can be done at any KBZ Bank branch.
Customer must have a bank account to link with Wedding Savings Account for monthly savings and pay out on maturity.

Deposit- The deposit can be made through a transfer of all (Ordinary/Cash) accounts (Savings, Call and Current)

Withdrawl- At the maturity date, customer shall be able to withdraw
interest compounded deposit amount from defined Pay Out Account.

Eligibility- Individual or Joint Account

Interest Calculation- The interest is calculated daily on the balance in the save for Wedding Savings Account and is compounded monthly.
