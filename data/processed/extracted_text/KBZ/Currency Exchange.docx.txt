Currency Exchange

Since the Central Bank of Myanmar (CBM) approved selected private banks to conduct foreign exchange operations starting from October 2011, Kanbawza (KBZ) Bank have operated the following foreign currencies in order to provide the more efficient flows of foreign trade and investments for the local and international customer alike.

Available Currency

US Dollars (USD)

Euro (EUR)

Singapore Dollar (SD)

Thai (Baht)

Foreign Exchange (FX) counters for (Petty Cash)

In its ongoing effort to cater to the foreign currency requirements of individuals traveling abroad for the following reasons, KBZ Bank is continuously selling up to 500 USD or its equivalent in foreign currency.

1. Offshore Job

2. Education

3. Medical Purposes

4. Pilgrimage

5. Attending meetings (invited by Foreign Governments/ Organizations)
