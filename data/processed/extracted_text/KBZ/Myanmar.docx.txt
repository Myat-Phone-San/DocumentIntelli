Myanmar-Thai Border Trade Services

Banking services for Myanmar-China Border Trade are now available at KBZ Bank. The services provided by KBZ Bank are as below:

Baht account opening

MMK account from/to Baht account (own account) direct transfer

Depositing to customer’s bank account after receiving funds for exported goods to Thailand

Remittance to a supplier in Thailand to make payment for the goods

Opening a Thai Baht Account at KBZ Bank

The following documents are required to open a Thai Baht account at KBZ Bank.

Fill in the KBZ account opening form

Valid company registration certificate

Company Extract from DICA

Company Constitution

Company Meeting Minutes and BOD Resolutions

Import and export registration certificate

NRC of account holders

Initial Deposit | MMK equivalent to Baht 100
Minimum Balance | Baht 100

Remittance of THB transferred by a buyer in Thailand to the customer’s bank account

Required Documents (before export)

Application form to deposit money to CNH account (with company letterhead)

Bill of sale/signed document of transaction agreement

Required Documents (after export)

Application form to deposit money to CNH account (with company letterhead)

Bill of sale/signed document of transaction agreement

Shipping documents (copy)

Export Declaration (ED) Allowed Shipment Notification

Transfer money from the Customer’s THB account at KBZ Bank to the supplier in Thailand

Required Documents (before import – Advance TT)

Telegraphic Transfer Application Form

Bill of sale/signed document of transaction agreement

Undertaking Letter (Guarantee that a Release Order Notification (ID) will be submitted when the product arrives)

Import license (if needed)

Required Documents (after import)

Telegraphic Transfer Application Form

Bill of sale/signed document of transaction agreement

Shipping documents (copy)

Import license (if needed)

Import Declaration (ID) Release Order Notification
