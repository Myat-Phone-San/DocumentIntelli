Group Goal SuBoo Account

KBZ Bank would like to introduce a new Recurring Deposit account where it could help the customer with the targeted saving solution between a family or a group of friends and corporate funds (such as employee bonuses, donation etc more). This account enables customers to contribute monthly funds from multiple accounts toward a financial goal. It also helps with monthly compounding to help savings grow faster.

Opening an Account

Whether you’re an individual or a corporate entity, you can open an account aligned with your savings goal at any KBZ Bank branch.

Customers must have a bank account to link with Group Goal SuBoo Account for monthly payment and payout on maturity. Bank Accounts can be opened under the following types.

Joint Account

Associate Account

Deposit and Withdrawal

The deposit can be made through a transfer of all (Ordinary/Cash) accounts (Savings, Call and Current)

Withdrawal

At the maturity date, customers shall be able to withdraw interest compounded deposit amount from defined Pay Out Account.

Benefit

Great savings option to attain your goal.

Able to save a steady monthly amount.

Guaranteed returns

Better rate of interest with Guaranteed returns flexible terms.

Achieve shared goals by saving with friends, family, or groups.

Flexible deposit (Contribute equally or in varying amounts as per group preferences), flexible allocation, and auto-debit transactions.

Initial Deposit- 30,000MMK

Minimum Balance - 30,000MMK

Interest Rate- 6 Months –  9.50%, 9 Months –  9.75%, 1 Year –  10.00%

Interest- Interest is calculated daily on the balance in the Group Goal SuBoo  Account and is compounded monthly.

Duration Option- 6 Months, 9 Months, 1 Year

Why choose a Group Goal SuBoo  Account?

Multiple Pay-In Accounts

Contributions can be made by multiple individuals from different accounts, or as an associate type, can deposit by monthly amount for the target amount in their corporate accounts

Contribution Options

Equal Amount: Each contributor deposits the same fixed amount.

Flexible Amount: Each contributor can deposit different amounts.
