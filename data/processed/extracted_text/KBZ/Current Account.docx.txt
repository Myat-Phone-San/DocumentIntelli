Current Account

Current Account (Local Currency)

Current Account (Local) is suitable for individual businessmen, merchants and Foreigners working locally.

Eaxi Account (Formerly Current Account – Individual)

Eaxi Account is the new name for the Current Account – Individual (Ordinary and Cash) now unveiled with new and exciting benefits.

Opening an account

You can open an individual account or co-account with more than a person

Account Facility

MPU Debit Card will be issued that can be linked to the Eaxi Account to withdraw or transfer money through the ATM. A cheque book can be requested if required.

All your banking in one place

Bank to bank service for collecting invoice and clearing accounts

Statements

All account holders receive official statements

Only designated account holders are allowed to sign the cheque

Transfers

You will be able to transfer or withdraw funds at all KBZ branches

Online banking

Get access to all of KBZ latest online banking services

Interest

This account is not eligible for interest

Withdrawal and deposit

No limit to the numbers of withdrawals or deposits you can make during bank opening hours

Initial Deposit

– 1,000 MMK (individual account)
– 10,000 MMK (joint/corporate account)

Minimum Balance

– 1,000 MMK (individual account)
– 10,000 MMK (joint/corporate account)

Account Maintenance Fees

500 MMK (per month)*

*For Eaxi Accounts inactive for 6 months or more

Apply for Cheque Books via KBZ m/iBanking

Easily apply for cheque books via KBZ Bank’s mobile and internet banking 24/7 at the comfort of your home.

After the application, the cheque books can be obtained by:

Doorstep delivery via courier: Available in selected townships of Yangon, Mandalay, and Naypyitaw Regions

Pickup at home branch: Available in all regions.

Processing Times

For branch pickup, your cheque book will be available for collection at your home branch on the next business day following your request. For requests made outside banking hours, it will be ready by the second business day.

For courier delivery, the cheque book will be delivered via courier within two business days of the request date.

Fees and Charges

MICR Cheque Fees – 300 MMK per leaf

Non MICR Cheque Fees – 200 MMK per leaf

Stamp Duty Fees – 5 MMK per leaf

*Effective as of April 1st, 2026

Delivery Fees by Townships (if applicable)

*All applicable fees, including cheque book issuance and delivery charges, will be automatically debited from your account.

Activating the Cheque Book

After acquiring your cheque book, it must be activated via KBZ m/iBanking by entering the number of the first cheque leaf in the activation field.
