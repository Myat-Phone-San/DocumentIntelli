KBZ Payroll Services

KBZ Payroll offers businesses a convenient and reliable way to disburse employee salaries, bonuses, and reimbursements whether through KBZ bank accounts or KBZPay wallets anytime, anywhere.

PayAdvance is a convenient financial service that enables employees to access up to 50% of their monthly salary before month-end. Available through KBZPay, the PayAdvance service is offered to salaried employees of our partner companies that have opted into the program. Employees can request PayAdvance starting from the 16th of each month via the “PayAdvance” option under the Finance & Loan Services section in the KBZPay app. A flexible service fee applies based on the amount requested.
