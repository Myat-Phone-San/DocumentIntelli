Local Remittance

KBZ Bank has the largest branch network in Myanmar with more than 500 branches covering all the 14 states. KBZ Bank is also connected to all the other local banks for remittance service using real time online network to transfer your funds. Contact your nearest branch for information on remittance service.

Sending Remittance

The sender can personally visit the nearest KBZ Bank branch with their original NRC to send money.

Withdrawal

The recipient must be 18 years of age or older and must personally visit the designated branch with their original NRC to withdraw the money.
