Bank Guarantee Deposit Account

The Bank Guarantee Deposit Account offered by KBZ Bank is tailored for customers seeking a dedicated account for bank guarantee purposes. It serves as a secure repository exclusively for transactions associated with bank guarantees.

Deposit and Withdrawal

Customers enjoy the convenience of cash deposits, withdrawals, and account transfers through the Bank Guarantee Deposit Account. Additionally, funds can be seamlessly transferred from other KBZ Bank accounts, including Ordinary Accounts and Cash Accounts, to fulfill the requirements of the bank guarantee.

Opening an Account

Customers may open an individual account, a joint account with another person, or a corporate account.

Passbook

Bank Guarantee Deposit Account holders are issued a passbook upon successful opening of the account.

Interest

This account accrues interest on a daily basis at a rate of 6% per annum. The interest is deposited on a quarterly basis.

Initial Deposit and Minimum Balance

This account can be opened by depositing 1,000 Kyats in Myanmar currency as an initial deposit. Likewise, the minimum amount to be kept in the account is 1,000 Kyats.
