KBZ MPU-UnionPay Credit Card

KBZ MPU-UnionPay Credit Card provides you with a gateway to the world of convenient and secure payments. As a leading payment card brand, UnionPay International (UPI) is synonymous with reliability and acceptance, making it one of the most important credit cards in Asia. With an extensive merchant network spanning over 160 countries, including 7,000 merchants in Myanmar, you can enjoy the benefits of UnionPay wherever you go. Additionally, Cardholders can make transactions by ‘tapping’ their card on a contactless POS terminal.
