KBZ QuickPay (Cash Collection Service)

“KBZ QuickPay” is a cash collection service serving as a medium between our corporate merchants and their clients. All funds will be collected by over 500 KBZ branches real time but also via KBZ online banking.

Features & Benefits!

Facilitating the company’s cash resources within control quickly and efficiently

Deposit by cash (or) by KBZ cheque (or) account to account transfer

Service fee can be paid by merchant or by customer

All transactions (OTC+ Online) history can be checked via merchant web portal

Real time notification via email & SMS

Daily statement reports by email (excel, csv)

Facility to connect merchant’s internal system by Direct API integration (QP Online only)

Standard deposit slip available at all KBZ branches

Customized deposit slips are allowed

Requirements to Sign up

Corporate Current Account (MMK/USD)

Master Agreement

Quick Pay SLA Contract
