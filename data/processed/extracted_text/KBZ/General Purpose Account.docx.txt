General Purpose Account

General Purpose Account is ideal for those saving money regularly for their big dream. By saving a fixed amount of money every month, customers can build up their savings and achieve their any dream. This a great option for those who want to save money regularly without having to worry about investing a large sum of money upfront. With this account, customers can choose their preferred terms with monthly saving amount and targeted saving goals that suits their budget and financial goals.

Benefits

Great savings option to attain your goal

Able to save monthly steady amount

Guaranteed returns

Better rate of interest with flexible terms

Features

Terms of Deposit and Interest- 10% 1 year *Effective as of September 1st, 2024

Saving Frequency- Monthly

Minimum Monthly Saving Amount- 30,000 MMK

Maximum Monthly Saving Amount- Unlimited

Product Highlights

Opening An Account- General Purpose account can be opened at any KBZ Bank branch. Customer must have a bank account to link with General Purpose Savings Account for monthly savings and pay out on maturity.

Deposit- The deposit can be made through a transfer of all (Ordinary/Cash) accounts (Savings, Call and Current).

Withdrawal- At the maturity date, customer shall be able to withdraw
interest compounded deposit amount from defined Pay Out Account

Eligibility- Individual or Joint Account or Corporate/Business & Association

Interest Calculation- The interest is calculated daily on the balance in the General Purpose account and is compounded monthly.
