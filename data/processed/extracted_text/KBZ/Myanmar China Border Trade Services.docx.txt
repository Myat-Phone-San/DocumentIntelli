Myanmar China Border Trade Services

Banking services for Myanmar-China Border Trade are now available at KBZ Bank. The services provided by KBZ Bank are as below:

Yuan account opening

MMK account from/to Yuan account (own account) direct transfer

Depositing to customer’s bank account after receiving funds for exported goods to China

Remittance to a supplier in China to make payment for the goods

Opening a Chinese Yuan Account at KBZ Bank

The following documents are required to open a Chinese Yuan account at KBZ Bank.

Fill in the KBZ account opening form

Valid company registration certificate

Company Extract from DICA

Company Constitution

Company Meeting Minutes and BOD ResolutionsImport and export registration certificate

NRC of account holders

Initial Deposit | MMK equivalent to CNH 30
Minimum Balance | CNH 30

Remittance of CNH transferred by a buyer in China to the customer’s bank account

Required Documents (before export)

Application form to deposit money to CNH account (with company letterhead)

Bill of sale/signed document of transaction agreement
