KBZ Home Loan: Your home loan is probably the biggest investment you’ll ever make. Find out everything you need to know before you take the decision. KBZ Home Loan can give you the peace of mind of knowing your repayments won’t change during the loan period. Get a special promotional 11% interest rate if you can make a down payment of 50% on your dream home. Available in a variety of terms, up to 25 years. The following conditions are:

Conditions | 
Loan Amount | Minimum 14 Million ; Maximum LTV 70%
Loan Tenor | 3 to 25 years
Interest Rate | If the down payment is 50% and above, the interest rate is 11%; If the down payment is 50% of below, the interest rate is 13%
Minimum Down Payment | Minimum 30% of Bank Assessor value

Conditions are subject to the Bank’s sole discretion in changing it without prior notice to customers.

Fees |

Stamp Duty | 0.5% of the loan amount

Myanmar nationals and expats married to Myanmar Nationals
