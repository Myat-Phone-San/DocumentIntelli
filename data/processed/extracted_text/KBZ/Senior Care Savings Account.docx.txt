Senior Care Savings Account

Our exclusive Senior Care Savings Account for customers aged 60 and above offers a higher interest rate and monthly compounding to help savings grow faster. Enjoy added benefits like priority service at branches to make banking more rewarding and hassle-free.

Opening an Account

Customers aged 60 and above can open the following types of accounts:

Individual Account

Joint Account

Deposit and Withdrawal

Deposit unlimited times during bank opening hours.

The account holder can make withdrawals at any KBZ branch.

Withdraw unlimited funds once a week.

Interest

1 billion MMK and above: 10% per annum

Below 1 billion MMK: 9% per annum

Interest is earned monthly, ensuring regular earning.
If no interest is withdrawn, the interest is compounded to your account.

Passbook

You will be issued with a passbook upon successful opening of an account.

Priority Queue

Get access to the priority queue when making over-the-counter transactions at KBZ branches

Online Banking

Get access to all of KBZ latest online banking services
