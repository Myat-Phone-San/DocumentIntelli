MPU Teen Debit Card

Teen Debit Card is used for students between 12 & under 18 age. This card allows young customers to make payments at online stores, mini marts, shopping centers and restaurants without the inconvenience of carrying cash around all the time.

Applying for a card: You can apply for an Explore Teens Card at any of our KBZ Bank branches accompanied by your guardian or parents.

Transfers: Easily transfer cash between KBZ Explore Debit Cards at our ATMs.

Deposits: Add money to your Debit Card at any KBZ branch.

Withdrawals: Make cash withdrawals at any of KBZ’s vast network of ATMs as well ATMs of other banks. Withdrawals can be made at ATMs 24 hours a day
