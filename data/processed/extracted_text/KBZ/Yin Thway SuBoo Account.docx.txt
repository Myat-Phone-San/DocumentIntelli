Yin Thway SuBoo Account

Yin Thway SuBoo Account is perfect for expecting mothers who want to have financial security and support during their maternity journey. This account enables you to save and worry less about the financial burden that comes with childbirth and nurturing a baby. Regularly saving allows expecting mothers to ease financial worries during their pregnancy journey without having to invest a large sum of money upfront. With this account, customers can choose their preferred terms with a regular monthly saving amount and targeted saving goals that suit their budget and financial goals.

Features

Terms of Deposit and Interest- 9.5% (6 months), 9.75% (9 months), 10% (1 year)

Saving Frequency- Monthly

Minimum Monthly Saving Amount- 30,000 MMK

Maximum Monthly Saving Amount- Unlimited

Product Highlights

Opening An Account- Account opening can be done at any KBZ Bank branch. Customers must have a bank account to link with Yin Thway SuBoo Account for monthly savings and pay out on maturity.

Deposit- The deposit can be made through a transfer of all (Ordinary/Cash) accounts (Savings, Call and Current).

Withdrawal- At the maturity date, customers shall be able to withdraw interest compounded deposit amount from defined Pay Out Account.

Flexibility- Customers can choose their preferred saving terms – 6 months, 9 months, or 1 year – depending on their own requirements.

Eligibility- Individual or Joint Account

Interest Calculation- The interest is calculated daily on the balance in the Yin Thway SuBoo Account and is compounded monthly.
