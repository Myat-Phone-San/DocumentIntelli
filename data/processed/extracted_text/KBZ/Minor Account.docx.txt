Minor Account

Minor Account is designed for individuals under the age of 18 and for parents saving for their child’s education. Opening this account is the perfect way to nurture your child’s saving habits building the perfect foundation for their future.

Educational

Teach your children the habits and values of savings money for their future

Transfer easily

As soon as your child turns 18, the account can easily be transferred into an adult Savings Account

Interest

This account is eligible for interest

The Minor Account deposit account with 10,000 Kyats and above are subject to earn

– 1 Billion MMK and above: 10% per annum
– Under 1 Billion: 9% per annum

*Effective as of June 1st, 2026

Withdrawal and deposit

No limit to the numbers of withdrawals or deposits you can make during bank opening hours. (At your local branch)

Deposit dates to be eligible for interest

1st-5th every month

Interest withdrawal months

End of March, June, September and December

Minimum Balance- 1,000 MMK
