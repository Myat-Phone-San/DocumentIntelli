New Cash Account

New Cash Account – Savings, Call and Current with Easy Cash Withdrawal

KBZ Bank has introduced a new, New Cash Account for customers to manage their finances conveniently and to withdraw cash up to the deposited amounts without limits. New Cash Accounts can be opened as:

Cash Account – Savings (interest rate up to 10% p.a)

Cash Account – Call (interest rate up to 7% p.a), and

Cash Account – Current.

To open a Cash Account – Savings, Call and Current, deposits can be made as cash credit at any KBZ Bank branch nationwide.

The following customers are eligible to open Cash Account – Savings, Call and Current

Businesses (Both DICA and NON-DICA)

Organizations (NGO, INGO, Universities, etc.)

Individuals – Myanmar citizens & Resident foreigners (with valid Visa)

Benefits of Cash Account – Savings

1 Billion MMK and above: 10% per annum
Under 1 Billion: 9% per annum
*Effective as of June 1st, 2026

Passbook – ‘CASH ACCOUNT – Savings’ stamped

Support in setting up mBanking to enable you to manage your money digitally, without visiting a branch

Benefits of Cash Account – Call

5 Billion MMK and above: 7% per annum
1 Billion MMK to under 5 Billion: 6.5% per annum 
Below 1 Billion MMK: 6% per annum 
*Effective as of December 1st, 2025

Specially designated passbook

Support in setting up Corporate iBanking and mBanking to enable you to manage your money digitally, without visiting a branch

Benefits of Cash Account – Current

Unlimited withdrawals of cash* up to the amount of cash deposited, subject to maintaining the minimum balance

Free transfers from this account to any other KBZ account

Perform non-cash debit transactions at any open KBZ branch nationwide

Free Business Debit and MPU cards

Support in setting up Corporate iBanking and mBanking to enable you to manage your money digitally, without visiting a branch

Apply for Cheque Books via KBZ m/iBanking

Easily apply for cheque books via KBZ Bank’s mobile and internet banking 24/7 at the comfort of your home.

After the application, the cheque books can be obtained by:

Doorstep delivery via courier: Available in selected townships of Yangon, Mandalay, and Naypyitaw Regions

Pickup at home branch: Available in all regions.

Processing Times

For branch pickup, your cheque book will be available for collection at your home branch on the next business day following your request. For requests made outside banking hours, it will be ready by the second business day.

For courier delivery, the cheque book will be delivered via courier within two business days of the request date.

Fees and Charges

MICR Cheque Fees – 300 MMK per leaf

Non MICR Cheque Fees – 200 MMK per leaf

Stamp Duty Fees – 5 MMK per leaf

*Effective as of April 1st, 2026

Delivery Fees by Townships (if applicable)

*All applicable fees, including cheque book issuance and delivery charges, will be automatically debited from your account.

Activating the Cheque Book

After acquiring your cheque book, it must be activated via KBZ m/iBanking by entering the number of the first cheque leaf in the activation field.
