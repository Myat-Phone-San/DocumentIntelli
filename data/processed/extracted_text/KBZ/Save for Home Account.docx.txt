Save for Home Account

Save for Home Account account is ideal for those saving money regularly for the down payment of the mortgage loan and to buy a house. By saving a fixed amount of money every month, customers can build up their savings and achieve their dream of owning a home. This a great option for those who want to save money regularly without having to worry about investing a large sum of money upfront. With this account, customers can choose their preferred terms with monthly saving amount and targeted saving goals that suits their budget and financial goals.

Maximum Monthly Saving Amount- Unlimited

Product Highlights

Opening an account- Save for Home Account can be opened at any KBZ Bank branch.Customer must have a bank account to link with save for mortgage for monthly savings and pay out on maturity.

Deposit- The deposit can be made through a transfer of all (Ordinary/Cash) accounts (Savings, Call and Current)

Withdrawl- At the maturity date, customer shall be able to withdraw
interest compounded deposit amount from defined Pay Out Account.

Eligibility- Individual or Joint Account (Non-Corporate).

Interest Calculation- The interest is calculated daily on the balance in the save for home account and is compounded monthly.
