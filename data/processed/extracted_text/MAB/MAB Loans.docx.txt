Better Salary Advance

The ‘Better Salary Advance’ program allows MAB Payroll users to access up to (50%) of their salary in advance with a (30) days repayment period.

Benefits

Salary Advance: Up to (50%) of monthly income

Tenure: Short-term financing for up to (30) days

Platform: User-friendly online application

Eligible Borrowers

Must be an MAB Payroll customer for a minimum of (6) months.

Applicant minimum monthly salary must have at least (200,000) MMK.

Must maintain a clean financial record with no outstanding dues or blacklisting at MAB.

Better Salary Loan

A specialized loan facility designed for employees receiving salary via MAB Payroll, offering credit of up to (2) times their current salary without the need for collateral.

Benefits

Get financial support of up to (2x) your current salary

Flexible repayment options ranging from (2) to (6) months

Experience a seamless and simple online application process

Eligible Borrowers

Payroll History: Must have been receiving salary through MAB Payroll for at least (6) consecutive months.

Minimum monthly salary must be (300,000) MMK.

Financial Integrity: Must not have any outstanding debts or be on the blacklist of MAB.

Credit Standing: Must have a clean credit history, with no record of defaults with MAB or any other financial institutions in the Credit Bureau Report.

Rates and Fees

Interest | 15% p.a.
Processing Fee | 10,000 MMK

Enjoy a flexible loan tenure of up to (5) years
