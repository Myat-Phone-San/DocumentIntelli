MAB MMQR

MAB MMQR is a secure and efficient digital payment solution that allows merchants to accept QR payments from all MMQR-compliant mobile wallets and banking apps, fully aligned with the national MMQR standard issued by the Central Bank of Myanmar. It provides instant payment acceptance directly to the merchant’s MAB account, supports easy onboarding for all business types, and ensures a smooth and trusted digital payment experience for both merchants and customers.
