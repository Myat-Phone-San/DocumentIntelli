Import Financing

Import financing provide importers with working capital for their import payments at competitive foreign exchange rate and help to manage exporter (seller’s) risks with our letter of credit; providing expert advice on documents under collections sight or usance.

Export Financing

Export financing provides exporters with working capital for pre-export (packing credit) before shipment or for post-export after shipment.Helps exporters to manage risk by advising on the documents under export LC negotiation and provide finance to exporters before payment from the importers’ Banks.
