Flexi Life Insurance

Flexi Life Insurance is a savings plan with coverage, offering flexible access to funds for life’s milestones and anticipated cash payouts during the policy period. Upon the maturity of the insurance policy, you will be entitled to receive the maturity benefits.
It supports your goals whether buying a home, building cash reserves, or planning for retirement while securing your future and your loved ones.

Benefits

Maturity Benefit Payout - The full value of the sum insured is payable in one lump sum upon the maturity date specified in the insurance policy

Survival Benefit Payout - receive cash payouts at specified policy durations as immediate, additional income during the policy term

Accumulation of Survival Benefit - Survival benefit payouts which are not withdrawn can be accumulated with an interest rate and paid out upon policy maturity

Death/Total Permanent Disability Benefits - Receive 100% of the sum insured in one lump sum, should the insured suffer Death or Total Permanent Disability

Features

Security – Build up your cash reserves while enjoying financial protection for your family.

Withdrawals– Receive payouts when you complete a designated number of policy years during the policy term, which can be cashed out, used for insurance premium payments or continue to accumulate for other uses later on and gain a bigger maturity amount.

Flexibility – Your financial needs may change over the course of your life – adjust your policy term, premium payment mode and how you would like to receive your survival benefit payouts according to your evolving needs.

Student Life Insurance

Now parents can plan for their child’s future, with Myanmar’s first student life insurance policy. This plan ensures that children’s education and future are protected, no matter what, if parent’s (policyholder) pass away or total permanent disability.

Benefits

Maturity Benefit Payout - Receive the full value of the sum insured over four equal installments beginning from the child’s 17th birthday

Full Premium Waiver Benefit - Full waiver on the outstanding premium payments, if the parent suffers from Death or Total Permanent Disability. The child will continue to receive the full value of the suminsured over four equal installments begniing from the child’s 17th birthday

Death Benefit - If the insured child loses his or her life due to unforeseen circumstances, the parent will receive the full value of the sum insured in one lump sum

Total Permanent Disability Benefit - If the insured child suffers from total permanent disability, the parent will receive the full value of the sum insured in one lump sum

Features

Note: All treatments must be performed at a hospital accredited by the Ministry of Health.

The first product of its kind in Myanmar, it now means any child between the ages of thirty days and twelve years old (measured by the age of the next birthday) can be covered.

This policy is paid for through a regular savings plan and provides insurance for both parent and child in the case of either death or total permanent disability.
